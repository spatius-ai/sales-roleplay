The hidden objection: every buyer has one real reason they might not buy, made up for each call. They will not say it if you only pitch.

How to find it: ask about how they work today, their plans and timing, and what could stop them. When you find it, this card turns green.

How it works
  1. 01Pick a buyer, a difficulty and what you sell.
  2. 02Talk. The buyer pushes back, and the interest meter moves with every answer.
  3. 03Ask good questions to find the hidden objection.
  4. 04End the call for your scorecard, with a better way to say your weakest line.
A tip

Ask before you pitch. Buyers open up when you ask how they work today, what is hard, what their timing is, and what could stop them. Pitching early makes the meter drop.
